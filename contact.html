Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I verify a visit?

Open the landmark's page while you're there and answer its 3-question quiz. The questions are about details you can only see in person. Get all three right to earn the stamp and XP.

Do I need an account?

No. You can explore and take quizzes as a guest. Create a free account on the Login page to keep your XP, stamps, badges and trip plans under your name. Anything you earned as a guest moves into your account when you sign up. Accounts are saved in this browser, so use the same device and browser to log in again.

Is the trip planner a real booking?

No. It builds a suggested schedule with timings and estimated costs. Tickets are bought at each landmark.

Are the opening hours accurate?

They're a guide and can change during Ramadan and public holidays. Check the official museum website before you go.
