Sharjah Heritage Museum

✅ Visited · +150 XP earned

About this landmark

Set in the heart of the Heart of Sharjah heritage district, the Sharjah Heritage Museum occupies the restored Bait Al Naboodah, a coral-stone family house built in the 1840s. Its rooms open onto a shaded courtyard, a design that kept families cool long before air conditioning.

Galleries cover the full cycle of Emirati life: birth and childhood, traditional medicine, poetry and oral storytelling, pearl diving, and the crafts that sustained coastal communities. Look out for the original wind tower, the carved teak doors and the hand-woven sadu textiles.

Allow around ninety minutes. The audio guide below covers the highlights, and the quiz at the bottom can only be answered by looking closely at what is on display.
